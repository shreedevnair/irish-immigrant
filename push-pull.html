Push Factors for Immigration

Many push factors for Irish immigration exist, but most notably were the Irish Potato Famine and the mass famine and mass loss of life that ensued

Pull Factors for Immigration

The California gold rush, a surplus of available jobs, and the vast newly opened frontiers in the west give many reasons for Irish immigrants to migrate to the US

Events during their immigration

Several events occur during the time of their immigration, such as the Irish potato famine and the lack of food that came with it, the California gold rush, and the ability to open up new business enterprises in the US
