How were the migrants treated and what did US citizens think of them?

There were many growing feelings of resentment of immigrants among natural-born US citizens, some of these feelings were against keeping immigration easy to do, and many Anti-Catholic feelings. many below middle-class Irish workers were paid with less than enslaved/newly freed workers

Were they able to get jobs and what were said jobs like?

Many of the jobs Irish immigrants were able to get as soon as they got off their boat were Police depts., Fire depts., and many industrial jobs. 1 Notable exception to this list was John F. Kennedy, who was of Irish descent and got the job of being the president of the United States

Were they able to be educated?

Not most 1st generation Irish immigrants were able to get an education, but 2nd and later generation Irish are able to get standard and higher level educations. Many funds were put up by churches and Irish Communities to make catholic schools

What were living conditions like?

Many Irish were poor and unemployed after finding a place in the US, which their homes were often overcrowded, and little to none facilities like sanitation and education

What was their financial situation like and did they make money?

Many Irish worked for extremely little, as said before sometimes fo less than minimum and newly freed slaves' wages

Did they have the same rights and freedoms?

Irishmen were limited the voting right in earlier colonies of the US, and during the 1850's many hate groups worked to try to limit the rights of Irish and other groups

Did they get what they left home for?

for some Irish, yes, but for many they got a portion of the profit, but discrimination and poor conditions negated many of the profit the Irish were able to get
